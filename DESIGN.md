# Bhikar Sawkar (भिकार सावकार) - Design System & Visual Direction

## Concept: "Midnight Table" (Buckshot-inspired Tabletop Hangout)
A moody, high-stakes underground hangout table inspired by tactile tabletop games like Buckshot and classic Indian late-night card sessions. Overhead warm incandescent spotlight casting over a deep green felt baize, cards with ivory linen textures, brass accents, and authentic Marathi card-table camaraderie.
