📜 Rules of Bhikar Sawkar (भिकार सावकार)

Bhikar Sawkar (भिकार सावकार), meaning Beggar–Moneylender, is a beloved traditional card game from Maharashtra played with friends and family during late-night hangouts and get-togethers.

🎯 Core Objective

Win every single card in the 52-card deck to become the Sawkar (सावकार). Players who run out of cards go bankrupt and are eliminated as Bhikar (भिकारी)!

🎴 Dealing & Hands

  • Standard 52-card deck (Jokers removed).
  • Cards are shuffled and dealt out evenly among all players face-down.
  • Players keep their cards face-down in their personal stack and are not allowed to look ahead.

⚡ Playing a Turn

  • Players take turns in clockwise order flipping the top card of their personal stack into the central pot.

🏆 Capturing the Central Pile (The Match Rule)

  • Whenever a player lays down a card that matches the same rank as the card immediately beneath it (e.g., a 7 on a 7, a King on a King, or an Ace on an Ace), that player wins the entire central pile!
  • The winner collects all cards, adds them to the bottom of their personal deck, and leads the next card to start the new pot.

🚫 Elimination & Crowning the Sawkar

  • When a player runs out of cards, they become Bhikar (भिकारी) and are knocked out.
  • The last player holding all 52 cards is crowned the undisputed Maha Sawkar (महा सावकार)!
